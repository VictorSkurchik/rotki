# Enforce a least-privilege companion scope

Mobile Access Sessions will be authorized by an Engine-enforced Companion Scope limited to self rename and Unpair, coherent Snapshot and bounded older-History reads, and explicitly approved global or per-source Refresh creation and observation under `/api/1/companion/*`. Pairing registration and Device Key challenge/proof are separate pre-session surfaces; Full Client Pairing and Device Session management retain their browser-cookie control realm. The method-and-route matrix is deny-by-default, `/device-sessions/current` derives its target only from the Access Session, and no Companion credential authenticates general `/api/1` endpoints. All other Profile administration, credentials, settings, export, and financial-action endpoints are denied regardless of what the UI exposes; adding a mobile capability requires an allowlist change plus positive and negative authorization tests.
