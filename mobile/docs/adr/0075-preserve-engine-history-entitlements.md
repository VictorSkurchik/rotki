# Preserve Engine History entitlements

The Companion Snapshot and older-History endpoint will expose consecutive slices of the same effective History Entitlement enforced by the Engine's existing Clients rather than creating a mobile bypass or a stricter mobile-only paywall. The Engine selects the newest allowed underlying history groups before filters, then returns complete curated display-group join closures; narrow filters cannot spend unused slots to reveal older data. Free, inactive, and unresolved premium state use the existing 1,000-group fallback, while a resolved active plan supplies its configured limit. Companion reads never contact the premium service, and an effective limit change alters History Generation and Snapshot Revision so active pagination restarts coherently. A terminal page distinguishes actual History exhaustion from `history_entitlement_limit` without exposing total counts or unstable plan names. This retains current product policy while allowing the new grouped read model to remain deterministic and honest about why older records are unavailable.
