# Model assets and liabilities as positive magnitudes

Every Balance Contribution in the Portfolio Snapshot will carry an explicit closed `category` of `asset` or `liability`. Amounts and every represented non-null valuation are canonical exact non-negative magnitudes; negative contribution values are rejected rather than treated as an alternative liability encoding, and the same asset may exist independently in both categories without premature netting. Overview exposes `known_assets_value`, `known_liabilities_value`, and `known_net_value`, with known net value equal to known assets minus known liabilities; only known net value may be negative. This preserves the Engine's established balance semantics, keeps debt presentation unambiguous across Android and iOS, and makes exact aggregation testable even when valuation is partial. Percentages and display signs are derived in shared or native presentation code and are not transported.
