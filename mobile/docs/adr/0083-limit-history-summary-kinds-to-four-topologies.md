# Limit History Summary kinds to four topologies

Snapshot schema version 1 will recognize exactly four `HistoryGroup.summary.kind` values: `swap`, `movement`, `bridge`, and `activity`. `swap` carries ordered `spend_event_ids`, `receive_event_ids`, and `fee_event_ids`; `movement` and `bridge` carry ordered `out_event_ids`, `in_event_ids`, and `fee_event_ids`; every variant also has the common `primary_event_id`. `activity` has no role arrays and is the fallback for staking, rewards, income, expense, DeFi, governance, mixed activities, multiple independent sub-activities, incomplete or ambiguous topology, and every future pattern not represented by version 1. Directional and fee arrays reference Events only and never copy their financial facts. The Engine classifies from proven entry and link topology rather than event labels alone. This deliberately avoids creating a second semantic taxonomy over the Engine's large, evolving type/subtype catalogue while retaining purpose-built native cards for the three stable multi-leg shapes.
