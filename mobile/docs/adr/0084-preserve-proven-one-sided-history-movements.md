# Preserve proven one-sided History movements

A version-1 `movement` or `bridge` History Summary may represent one proven directional leg: at least one of `out_event_ids` or `in_event_ids` is non-empty, while the other may be an empty array. The Engine assigns the specialized kind only from a typed Asset Movement or typed bridge leg after Curated History projection; a generic event type/subtype, note, address, protocol label, or Client heuristic is insufficient. A two-sided Summary requires an exact persisted movement or bridge relation and never fabricates a counterpart Event from metadata. By contrast, `swap` requires at least one spend and one receive because either side alone cannot describe an exchange; an incomplete or ambiguous swap becomes `activity`. Fee arrays may be empty, but a fee alone cannot establish any specialized kind. This keeps unmatched deposits, withdrawals, and bridge legs useful in native UI without weakening the contract's no-guessing rule.
